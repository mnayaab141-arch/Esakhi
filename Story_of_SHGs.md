## From Her Hands to Your Heart ❤️

*Every handmade product carries a story of courage, creativity, and independence.*

Meet Sita, a woman from a small town in Madhya Pradesh. Like many women in her community, she had creative skills but limited opportunities to earn an income of her own.

Her journey changed when she joined a Self-Help Group (SHG) with other women in her community. Together, they began making beautiful bangles, jewellery, and traditional dupattas using their skills, patience, and creativity.

Initially, reaching customers beyond their local neighbourhood was difficult. Their products were made with care, but finding the right market was a challenge.

With the support of her group, Sita continued improving her craft and learning how to present and sell her products. Every order became a reason to feel proud, and every sale brought her one step closer to financial independence.

Today, her work represents more than handmade products. It represents the strength of women working together, supporting their families, preserving traditional skills, and building a brighter future.
